How I drove a 6.6% Home CTR lift via product grid redesign

A condensed product grid that makes more products immediately visible with high level descriptions spoke to a broader audience and provided enough information to encourage meaningful funnel progression

Showing more products above the fold — with brief descriptions — drove a 6.6% lift in homepage CTR.

Return to winners Contact me

The Challenge

The homepage product grid showed too few plans at a glance

The original UHOne homepage displayed a spacious product grid with oversized cards, meaning only 2–3 plans were visible before users had to scroll. Users arriving with general health insurance interest couldn't quickly scan the full range of available products. Those who didn't immediately recognise a relevant plan were likely to disengage before exploring further. The core question was whether condensing the grid — showing more options simultaneously with brief descriptions — would widen top-of-funnel appeal and reduce early drop-off.

Hypothesis

If we condense the product grid cards to display more plans above the fold with short descriptions, then homepage Click-Through Rate will improve because users who can immediately scan a broader range of products are more likely to find a relevant entry point before losing interest.

Key Insight

💡

Users didn't need bigger cards — they needed more options visible at once. Reducing the visual real estate per card while adding brief descriptors satisfied both the "scanning" behaviour of new visitors and the "comparing" behaviour of returning ones. This confirmed that information density, when organised well, outperforms white-space-heavy minimalism for product-discovery pages.

This test directly preceded the mobile product grid re-introduction test, which applied the same density principle to the mobile homepage and delivered a 4.5% Quote Start Rate lift.
