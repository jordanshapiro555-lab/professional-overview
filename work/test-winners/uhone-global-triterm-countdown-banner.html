How I drove a 14.1% global lift in high margin product cart additions

A countdown timer is a classic tactic for implementing urgency, especially when the urgency is genuine, not manufactured

A genuine countdown to a real product deadline — no manufactured scarcity — drove a 14.1% lift in cart additions for UHOne's highest-margin plan.

Return to winners Contact me

The Challenge

A sunsetting high-margin product had no urgency signal — and users had no reason to act

UHOne's TriTerm product — a high-margin, short-term health plan — was approaching a regulatory sunset date at the end of the enrollment period. It was one of UHOne's most valuable plans in terms of margin, meaning every additional TriTerm sale before its discontinuation represented outsized revenue. But users browsing the site had no awareness of the approaching deadline. Without any urgency signal, users who were considering TriTerm were deferring decisions, selecting other products, or simply not completing their purchase before the window closed. The challenge was to surface this real, time-sensitive context in a way that felt informative rather than manipulative.

Hypothesis

If we add a globally visible countdown banner displaying the days remaining to purchase TriTerm, then TriTerm cart additions will increase because a visible, genuine deadline motivates users who are already considering the product to act before the opportunity closes, without resorting to manufactured scarcity.

Test Design

PagesAll UHOne pre-shop pages (global, TTM-targeted)
DevicesDesktop & Mobile
AudienceAll pre-shop visitors within TriTerm enrollment window
Traffic Split50/50 control / variant
Duration~3 weeks (within enrollment deadline window)

Key Insight

💡

Urgency tactics are effective in direct proportion to how genuine the urgency is. The TriTerm sunset was a real, non-manufactured deadline — which made the banner credible rather than gimmicky. Users who were already evaluating TriTerm needed only the reminder that time was limited to convert. The neutral overall cart additions result confirms the lift was concentrated on the target segment without cannibalising other products, validating the TTM targeting strategy.
