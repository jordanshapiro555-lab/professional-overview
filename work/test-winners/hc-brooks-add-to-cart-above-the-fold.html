How I drove a 3.42% cart adds lift by moving Add to Cart above the fold

Anchoring the primary Add to Cart CTA higher on mobile PDPs helped Brooks Brothers capture purchase intent before users had to scroll or re-orient around the page.

The recommended variation delivered a statistically significant 3.42% lift in cart adds per visitor while keeping down-funnel risk lower than the more aggressive hidden-price treatment.

Return to winners Contact me

The Challenge

Mobile shoppers had purchase intent, but the primary action was not immediately anchored in view

On mobile product detail pages, users often arrive with high purchase intent but still need a clear, immediate next step. When the Add to Cart action is not anchored above the fold, the shopper has to spend extra effort finding the conversion path. That extra effort can dilute intent at the most valuable point in the funnel.

Hypothesis

If we anchor Add to Cart above the fold on mobile PDPs, then cart adds per visitor will improve because users will be able to act on purchase intent sooner and with less visual effort.

Key Insight

All variations increased cart additions, but the lowest-risk recommendation was the simpler above-the-fold CTA. The variant that also hid title and price produced the largest cart-add lift, but it introduced a possible order-rate tradeoff. For rollout, the safer win was capturing intent with a clearer CTA while preserving product information shoppers need before purchase.
