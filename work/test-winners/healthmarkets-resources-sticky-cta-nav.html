How I drove a 143% Agent Start Page lift via a Resources sticky CTA nav

Surfacing sticky conversion CTAs on the HealthMarkets resources section dramatically improved navigation to high-value funnels, with a 143% lift in Agent Start Page visits and 46% lift in Quote Start Rate

Adding a sticky CTA to the resources section captured intent from high-value content readers — driving the largest lift in this entire portfolio: +143% Agent Start.

Return to winners Contact me

The Challenge

High-intent content readers had no path to convert after finishing an article

The HealthMarkets resources section — articles, guides, and insurance education content — attracted some of the site's most motivated users. These were people actively researching their health insurance options, investing real time in understanding their choices. Yet the section offered no conversion pathway. After reading an article that might have fully convinced them to seek expert advice, users had no visible next step. They would finish reading and leave, despite being at peak purchase intent. The section was an unmonetised acquisition asset sitting right in the middle of the funnel.

Hypothesis

If we add sticky conversion CTAs specifically to the resources section pages, then Agent Start Page visits and Quote Start Rate from resources visitors will increase dramatically because users who have just consumed educational content are at peak purchase intent and need only a low-friction trigger to move into the conversion funnel.

Key Insight

💡

The 143% lift — by far the largest in this portfolio — confirms that context matters enormously in CTA placement. Users actively reading content about health insurance are already in a decision-making mindset; they simply needed a visible exit ramp. This is a textbook example of intent matching: placing conversion mechanisms at the exact moment of maximum motivation. Time-on-page being unaffected proves the CTA didn't distract from content consumption — it simply added a next step that was previously missing.

The massive lift here validated the resources section as an under-monetised acquisition channel and prompted a broader content strategy investment across the site.
