How I improved ordering by surfacing saved bags on the homepage

Brooks Brothers shoppers who arrived on the homepage converted three times better than users landing deeper in the funnel on PDPs and PLPs, so we made homepage ordering easier for high-intent return visitors.

The saved bag callout was especially effective for users typing the Brooks Brothers home URL directly into the browser or returning from browser history.

Return to winnersContact me

The Challenge

High-intent homepage visitors needed a clearer path back to their saved bag

Many homepage arrivals were returning shoppers who came directly to the Brooks Brothers home URL or loaded it from browser history. The homepage needed a direct recovery path that helped them resume ordering instead of forcing them to rediscover products through navigation or search.

Hypothesis

If we surface a saved bag callout on the Brooks Brothers homepage, then revenue per visitor will improve because returning shoppers can resume ordering immediately from the entry point where they most often return.

Test Design

PagesBrooks Brothers homepage
DevicesDesktop & Mobile
AudienceHomepage visitors, especially direct and browser-history return traffic

Results

Home arrivals converted 3x better than PDP/PLP landings

Key Insight

The homepage can be a lower-funnel surface when users return through direct navigation or browser history. The saved bag callout matched returning shoppers' likely intent: pick up where they left off.
