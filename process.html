Roadmapping

Strategic
Planning
Overview

  1. Defining Goals

    Understanding what success looks like to ensure strategy and initiatives map to business goals.

  2. External Trends And Opportunity Audit

    Overview of the current market, potential changes, and competitor behavior to stay ahead in the industry.

  3. Internal Performance And Opportunity Audit

    How are current actions supporting goals, and how can we improve those activities or add new ones to enhance performance?

  4. Road-mapping

    Consolidated roadmap view of proposed deliverables and their dependencies to provide a clear path forward.

Concepting

Test
Concepting
Overview

  1. Hypothesis Development

    Formulating clear, research-backed hypotheses that connect identified friction points to measurable outcomes.

  2. Wireframing

    Creating detailed mockups for each test variant to clearly communicate the proposed experience before build.

  3. Scope And Success Metrics

    Defining test parameters, audiences, traffic splits and split methodologies, duration estimates, and primary and guardrail metrics for each concept.

AI in the Process

Powered by AI

How AI accelerates every phase

What used to take weeks can now be done within hours — if not minutes.

  1. 01

    Roadmapping

    AI serves as a thought partner to parse through analytics exports, competitors, and qualitative insights — identifying key points of funnel friction with supporting data points, faster than any manual audit.

  2. 02

    From Strategy to High-Fidelity Mocks

    Once strategy is validated, a custom CRO agent creates image generation prompts that feed into a separate image generation agent — delivering high-fidelity mockups from just an idea, ready for stakeholder review.

  3. 03

    Mocks to Testable Experiments

    Mocks are fed to a coding AI along with relevant HTML, functionality requirements, and testing platform specs — delivering production-ready, testable experiments without a lengthy development cycle.
