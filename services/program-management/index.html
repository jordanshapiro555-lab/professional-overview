Program Management

Scale experimentation with a reliable operating system

If your tests are slow, blocked, or inconsistently analyzed, program management closes the execution gap. I run the operating cadence that keeps experiments shipping and learning compounding.

  • Sprint planning, sequencing, and dependencies
  • QA standards and launch readiness checks
  • Weekly leadership reporting tied to KPIs

Best fit

Primary outcome: faster cycle times with higher analysis quality and clearer business alignment.
