Frequently asked questions

How long does a CRO audit take?

Most audits are delivered in 2–3 weeks, depending on site complexity and how quickly analytics access is provisioned. I will give you a firm timeline during our kickoff call.

Can you audit one funnel instead of the whole site?

Yes. Focused audits are available for signup flows, checkout funnels, quote request paths, or lead-form journeys. A narrower scope means faster delivery and deeper analysis on the flow that matters most.

What analytics access do you need?

Viewer access to GA4 (or your primary analytics platform), your A/B testing tool, and heatmap or session recording tools if available. I will send a detailed access checklist before we start.

Will my team get test ideas we can launch immediately?

Yes. Every recommendation is delivered as a test-ready hypothesis with wireframe direction, success metrics, and sample-size estimates—not high-level observations your team still has to interpret.

How is this different from an agency audit?

Agency audits are often templatized and lead to a retainer pitch. This audit is independent, evidence-based, and designed so your team can execute the roadmap without ongoing dependency on me.
