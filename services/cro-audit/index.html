Why buyers choose this audit

The point is not to collect more observations. It is to give your team the evidence and sequencing needed to make better conversion decisions.

01

Know what is actually blocking conversion

I look at the journey the way a web product owner should: traffic quality, page intent, analytics signal, user effort, trust, messaging, and the handoff to the next step.

02

Prioritize the work worth shipping first

Every recommendation is weighed by likely impact, confidence, effort, and dependency. You get a ranked action plan, not a long list that leaves your team arguing.

03

Give stakeholders a decision-ready plan

The output is written for action: what the issue is, why it matters, what evidence supports it, and how to turn it into an experiment or product improvement.

What you get in a CRO audit

A clear diagnostic package for teams that need to move from hunches to an informed roadmap.

1

Funnel and analytics readout

A practical review of conversion paths, drop-off points, event quality, and segment patterns so the team understands what the data can and cannot prove.

2

UX and messaging friction map

A page-by-page diagnosis of where visitors lose clarity, confidence, or momentum, with notes your design, copy, and product teams can use.

3

Prioritized opportunity roadmap

A ranked set of fixes, tests, and measurement improvements organized by business value, confidence, effort, and sequencing.

4

Executive action briefing

A concise narrative that explains the biggest constraints, what to do next, and where execution should begin.

CRO audit questions, answered

What does a CRO audit include?

A CRO audit includes analytics and funnel review, UX and messaging diagnosis, opportunity prioritization, and a clear action plan for experiments or product improvements.

Who is this CRO audit for?

It is for growth, marketing, ecommerce, SaaS, education, and product teams that have traffic but need a sharper view of what is blocking conversion.

What data helps the audit?

Useful inputs include GA4 or another analytics platform, testing history, heatmaps, session recordings, key landing pages, product pages, forms, or checkout flows.

Is implementation included?

The audit is designed to make execution easier, but implementation can be scoped separately. Some teams use the roadmap internally; others bring me in to help run the next phase.

What happens after the audit?

You receive a prioritized roadmap that can feed experiments, UX updates, measurement fixes, and stakeholder decisions. Implementation support can be scoped separately.
