Signs this is the right starting point

Keyword mapping is most useful when your search demand is real, but your page coverage or routing logic is creating friction before the visitor even starts.

I

Important queries are landing on generic pages. The page is technically relevant, but not specific enough for the searcher's intent.

C

CPC keeps rising without better conversion quality. You need to know whether the page experience is wasting expensive demand.

S

SEO and paid search disagree on page ownership. Teams need one clear map for which page should serve each demand cluster.

N

High-volume terms do not have a natural destination. That is often a page gap, not just a campaign structure problem.

How the re-mapping process works

The workflow starts with page understanding, moves into query and performance validation and ends with implementation and new page opportunities.

1

Build the page and keyword universe

  1. Scrape all potential landing pages' HTML and pass it to AI to create mutually exclusive, relevant keyword sets for each page.
  2. Export campaign query data and Google Ads query data to identify high-volume keywords plus current and potential mapping.
  3. Identify keywords that are currently mapped to the wrong page.
2

Validate and move the right terms

  1. Evaluate CPC and CVR on both the suboptimal mapping and ideal state mapping to validate semantic fit.
  2. Re-map the validated keywords in platform.
  3. Evaluate MoM performance deltas while accounting for seasonality and other in-platform changes.
3

Turn page gaps into opportunities

  1. Identify keywords that do not map well to any existing pages.
  2. Validate volume for opportunity sizing.
  3. Identify unserved, high-volume keyword clusters as new page opportunities.

Keyword mapping questions, answered

What is keyword analysis and mapping?

Keyword analysis and mapping is the process of matching search terms to the best landing page based on intent, relevance, current performance, conversion potential and available page coverage.

Who is this service for?

It is for paid search, SEO, ecommerce, SaaS, education and lead generation teams that have keyword volume but suspect traffic is landing on pages that do not match user intent.

What data do you need?

Useful inputs include landing page URLs, campaign query data, Google Ads search term data, SEO query data, CPC, CVR, spend, conversions and any current keyword-to-page mapping rules.

How do you validate a keyword should move?

I compare semantic fit, current performance, ideal page performance and business value. CPC and CVR help show whether the current page is wasting demand or whether the better-matched page can capture it more efficiently.

Can this identify new landing pages?

Yes. When high-volume keyword clusters do not map cleanly to any existing page, they become candidates for new page creation, content expansion or campaign structure changes.
