Signs personalization will move the needle for you

Personalization works when you have the right conditions. Here's how to know if you're ready.

⚠️

Conversion is flat despite growing traffic — you are acquiring diverse audiences but serving them all the same experience.

⚠️

You have CRM or behavioral data you are not using — rich audience signals exist but nothing on-site changes based on them.

⚠️

Your personalization tool is shelfware — you bought the platform but never built a strategy or measurement framework around it.

⚠️

Past personalization was "spray and pray" — you targeted dozens of micro-segments with no holdout testing and no idea what actually worked.

⚠️

Returning visitors convert at the same rate as new ones — there is no recognition, continuity, or progressive disclosure for known users.

⚠️

Campaign landing pages feel generic — paid traffic from different sources and intents all land on the same page with the same message.

Personalization framework

A disciplined four-step process that eliminates wasted personalization effort and focuses on proven lift.

1

Find signal-rich audiences

Start with segments where intent and needs are clear—returning visitors, campaign cohorts, lifecycle stages, high-value referrers. Quantify the conversion gap to justify the effort before building anything.

2

Design for known needs

Change message, social proof, offer, or flow based on what the segment demonstrably needs—not assumptions. Each experience brief explains the behavioral rationale and expected impact.

3

Validate with controlled tests

Run every personalized experience against the default in a proper A/B test with holdout groups. Only experiences that produce statistically significant lift graduate to production.

4

Scale and compound

Winning experiences get hardcoded, monitored for decay, and extended to adjacent segments and pages. Learnings feed the next round of hypothesis generation.

Frequently asked questions

What personalization platforms do you work with?

I am platform-agnostic and have worked with Optimizely, Dynamic Yield, Adobe Target, VWO, and custom solutions. The strategy is tool-independent—great personalization starts with audience understanding, not software.

How many segments should we personalize for?

Start with 2–3 high-signal segments. More segments means more maintenance and thinner traffic per variant. The goal is validated lift in your most impactful audiences first, then expand.

How do you avoid false positives with personalization tests?

Every personalized experience is tested against the default with proper sample sizing, holdout groups, and segment-level runtime controls. Novelty effects are monitored and results are only called after reaching statistical significance.

What if we don't have a personalization tool yet?

We can start with your existing A/B testing tool for segment-targeted experiments. I will help evaluate whether a dedicated personalization platform is justified based on the opportunity size and your team's capacity.

How long before we see results?

Audience analysis and strategy take 2–3 weeks. First experiments launch in weeks 3–5. You should have validated winners within 6–8 weeks of engagement start, depending on traffic volume.
