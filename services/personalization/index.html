Personalization Service

Personalization that earns its keep with measurable lift

Most personalization programs create complexity without business impact. This service focuses only on high-signal audiences and validates every experience with controlled experimentation.

Core deliverables

  • Audience opportunity sizing and prioritization
  • Message and offer strategy by segment
  • A/B testing against default experience
  • Scale plan for validated winners

Best fit

Teams with meaningful traffic and identifiable audience signals (CRM, behavior, source, lifecycle).

Primary outcome: higher conversion in key segments without hurting baseline performance.

Personalization framework

Find signal-rich audiences

Start with segments where intent and needs are clear (returning visitors, campaign cohorts, repeat users).

Design for known needs

Change message, proof, or flow based on what the segment needs now—not assumptions.

Validate and scale

Run controlled experiments first. Only scale experiences that improve conversion and downstream KPIs.
