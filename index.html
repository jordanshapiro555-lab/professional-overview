Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about CRO and working together.

What is Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)?

CRO is the process of increasing the percentage of website visitors who take a desired action — purchasing, signing up, or requesting a quote — through data-driven research and A/B testing. Instead of spending more to acquire new traffic, CRO makes your existing traffic work harder.

What industries do you specialize in?

My deepest experience is in healthcare insurance (UnitedHealthcare, HealthMarkets), education (DeVry), retail and apparel (Aeropostale, Brooks Brothers, Nautica), and health technology (Hear.com). These are high-stakes industries where conversion improvements translate directly into significant, measurable revenue.

What A/B testing tools and analytics platforms do you use?

I work with Optimizely, Adobe Target, and Statsig for experimentation, and GA4, Adobe Analytics, Domo, Mixpanel, Quantum Metrics, Hotjar, and Mouseflow for analytics. I'm tool-agnostic and can adapt to your existing stack.

What ROI can I expect from a CRO program?

My programs average 450% ROI. The best result was 1061% ROI for UHOne's 2024 program ($8.7M in attributed revenue). Results vary by traffic volume and baseline conversion rate.

How does the CRO engagement process work?

Engagements follow a Plan → Build → Run & Analyze cycle. Each phase covers the full loop — from analytics audit and hypothesis development through creative build, QA, launch, and statistical reporting. Each iteration compounds learning and impact.

How quickly can I see CRO results?

Most clients see validated learnings within 2–4 weeks per test cycle. Quick-win projects — like DeVry's search redirect optimization — can produce measurable revenue impact within the first 30 days.

Do you offer website personalization services?

Yes — personalization is a core service. I target users by demographics, behavior, traffic source, or purchase intent to deliver tailored experiences that lift conversion for specific segments.
